Frequently Asked Questions

The cannabis excise duty is a tax imposed by the Government of Canada on cannabis products produced in Canada. It applies to dried/fresh cannabis, edibles, extracts, and topicals.
The cannabis licensee (producer) who packages the cannabis product is responsible for remitting the excise duty to the Canada Revenue Agency (CRA).
The duty is the higher of a flat rate based on quantity (e.g., per gram of flower or mg of THC) and an ad valorem rate (a percentage of the sales). The specific rates vary by product type and province/territory.
